How it works

  • Runs locally. TubeForge installs on your Windows PC. Your channels, keys and data stay on your computer.
  • You approve every step. Agents prepare the work; nothing is uploaded or published until you approve it.
  • Your own accounts. You connect your own YouTube channel and free AI keys. Google sign-in is used only for your channels.
  • Built for original content. TubeForge is designed around your own insight and voice, and checks for reused or misleading content before upload.
  • Honest numbers. Scores are estimates based on cited sources. TubeForge never predicts views, subscribers or earnings.
