How it works

  • Runs locally. TubeForge installs on your Windows PC. Your channels, keys and data stay on your computer.
  • You approve every step. Agents prepare the work; nothing is uploaded or published until you approve it.
  • Your own accounts. You connect your own YouTube channel and free AI keys. Google sign-in is used only for your channels.
  • Built for original content. TubeForge is designed around your own insight and voice, and checks for reused or misleading content before upload.
  • Honest numbers. Scores are estimates based on cited sources. TubeForge never predicts views, subscribers or earnings.

How client onboarding will work

  1. Apply: tell us about your agency or your channel plans.
  2. Licence and branding: we set up your licence and your name, logo and colours.
  3. Install: a guided Windows setup on the client's PC (about 30 minutes).
  4. Connect the channel: the client signs in with Google and runs a private test upload.
  5. Find the niche: TubeForge scores channel ideas with evidence; the client approves a blueprint.
  6. Produce and publish: videos are prepared step by step, and nothing goes live without the client's approval.

Questions

  • Do I need a server? No. TubeForge runs on a normal Windows PC (8 GB RAM is enough).
  • What does it cost to run? It uses free AI tiers and a local model by default. Paid services stay off unless you switch them on yourself.
  • Will it promise views or income? No. Nobody can honestly promise that. TubeForge shows estimates with sources and helps you make original videos that follow YouTube's rules.
  • When can I buy it? When TubeForge is complete. Join the waitlist and we will contact you first.
