Privacy Policy

TubeForge (personal) · last updated [DATE]

TubeForge is a personal, self-hosted YouTube production assistant. This copy is installed and operated by [OWNER NAME] (“I”, “the owner”) on my own Windows computer, for my own YouTube channels only. I am the developer of this personal install for the purposes of the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. There are no other users and no customers.

1. What YouTube and Google data TubeForge accesses

TubeForge uses the YouTube API Services. It is authorised by me, with Google sign-in, for my own channels:

DataWhyGoogle permission
My channel's ID, title and handleTo confirm I connected the right channelyoutube.readonly

TubeForge does not read other people's private data, does not scrape YouTube or Google, does not download YouTube videos and does not combine data from channels I do not own.

2. Where the data is stored

  • Everything is stored locally on my computer in a PostgreSQL database and data folder. Nothing is sent to a TubeForge server: there is none.
  • Google sign-in tokens, the OAuth client secret and upload session links are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key kept in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Data from owner-initiated look-ups of public YouTube items is kept for at most 30 days. Stored data about my own channels is re-authorised at least every 30 days. Data for videos that no longer exist on YouTube is deleted.

3. Sharing

YouTube API data is not sold or shared with third parties. TubeForge may send text (never YouTube audiovisual content, never sign-in tokens) to AI providers that I choose to enable, and only under their own terms. AI providers enabled in this install: Google Gemini API, free tier (Gemini API terms; on the free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products, so no private data is sent to it).

4. Cookies and local storage

The TubeForge dashboard runs at http://127.0.0.1:8765 on my computer. It uses one sign-in cookie (tf_session), one anti-forgery cookie (tf_csrf) and browser local storage for display preferences (theme, chosen channel). No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

5. Google's privacy policy

Use of YouTube is subject to the Google Privacy Policy (also at www.google.com/policies/privacy) and the YouTube Terms of Service.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • In TubeForge, Channels → Disconnect revokes the token at Google, deletes it at once, and deletes all stored YouTube API data of that channel within 7 days. Delete API data does the deletion without disconnecting.
  • If TubeForge sees that access was revoked, it stops using the data and deletes it within 7 days.
